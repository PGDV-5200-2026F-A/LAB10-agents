Data: seaborn-data/penguins.csv (mwaskom, 2021) · Stats: pen-gem/phase1_stats.py · Missing values are dropped per-feature (pairwise complete observations).

Reproduction

  • Source CSV: data/penguins.csv (fetched from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mwaskom/seaborn-data/master/penguins.csv).
  • python3 pen-gem/convert_data.py data/penguins.csv data/penguins.json
  • python3 pen-gem/phase1_stats.py data/penguins.json pen-gem/derived.json
  • Serve the repo root with python3 -m http.server and open /pen-gem/index.html.
  • D3 7.9.0 loaded from jsDelivr CDN (no local node_modules in this lab).
